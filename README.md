# About Me

I'm **Sundaralingam Duraisamy**, a **Final-Year B.Tech Computer Science and Business Systems** undergraduate with industry experience developing **AI-powered software systems** for enterprise use cases.

My primary focus is on building **production-ready AI applications** that combine **Large Language Models (LLMs), Agentic AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), FastAPI, Computer Vision, and scalable backend engineering**. I enjoy transforming research concepts into practical products that solve real-world business problems.

Through my internships, I've worked on intelligent automation for the automotive industry, multimodal AI systems, semantic search pipelines, backend APIs, and enterprise-grade AI workflows. My approach emphasizes software engineering best practices, maintainable architectures, and scalable deployment rather than experimental prototypes.

I continuously explore emerging AI technologies while strengthening my expertise in backend systems, cloud platforms, distributed applications, and modern software architecture.
